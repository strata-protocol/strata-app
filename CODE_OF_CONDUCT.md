# Code of conduct

> **UNAUDITED TESTNET SOFTWARE.** Strata and this repository are unaudited
> software running on Stellar testnet. This code of conduct applies to the
> project's community and repositories regardless.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this:

- Being accurate, and correcting yourself when you were wrong
- Assuming good faith, and asking before concluding
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected, and learning from it
- Focusing on what is best for the project and for the people reading it
- Being patient with people learning, especially around money-handling code

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing or distorting someone's stated experience because it is
  inconvenient
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on others to disengage
- Any conduct that would be unacceptable in a professional setting

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## A note on technical disagreement

Substantive disagreement about the code is expected and welcome. On this project
it usually has a specific shape: a claim that a contract does not do what a
document says, or that a number in the app disagrees with the contract.

Those are worth pursuing hard, and the way to do that is with evidence — a
ledger sequence, a simulation, a worked number from the spec. "The docs are
wrong" is a fine thing to claim; "the docs are wrong, here is the call that
proves it" is what gets it fixed. Attacking the person making the claim does
not.

Disagreeing about scope is also fine. "This should not be merged yet, and here
is why" is a legitimate review outcome, and it should be given plainly.

Reports are handled by the maintainers. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this code of conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by the other maintainers.

### Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of any
action they deem in violation of this code of conduct:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning, with clarity about the nature of
   the violation and why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may
   be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including
   no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban.** From any interaction with the project for a specified
   period.
4. **Permanent ban.** From any public interaction with the project.
